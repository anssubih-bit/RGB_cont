Bluetooth not available in this browser

On iPhone, Safari and Chrome can't talk to Bluetooth devices from a web page. Copy this page's link and open it in the free Bluefy browser from the App Store. On Android or a computer, use Chrome or Edge.

Can't find your strip?
  1. It will never show in iPhone Settings › Bluetooth. That's normal for LED controllers. Connect from this page.
  2. Force-quit LotusLanternX (swipe it away). The strip accepts only one phone at a time.
  3. Unplug the strip's power for 5 seconds, plug it back in, then tap Connect.
  4. Still nothing? Show all nearby devices and pick the one named ELK-BLEDOM, LEDBLE or similar.
